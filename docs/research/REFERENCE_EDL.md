# The reference edit, measured (the bar for the talk-reel engine)

The owner scored a hand-briefed AI edit of his 155 s crypto talking-head reel 5/10, and ours 1/10.
This file is that edit taken apart: what it kept, what it cut, what it showed, and how it sounds.
It is the bar the engine is rebuilt against — foundation first, finish second.

How it was measured: both audio tracks at 8 kHz, 24 log bands every 10 ms, each 1 s window of the
edit matched against the whole source (correlation 0.7–0.85 on every kept run); kept runs mapped
to the source transcript word by word. Graphics catalogued from a frame every 2 s and full-size crops.

Rule taken: **every idea is said once, in its most complete telling; the ask is made once, at the end;
the opening is the strongest concrete claim; false starts and fragments go.** No pause of 0.3 s or
more survives (the source had 38, ours kept 6).

## 2. The pictures: one design system, one graphic per sentence, in the top band

Every graphic sits in the top 30 % over the head; the speaker is never covered and never replaced.
No full-frame stage, no effect, no background swap. One look throughout: dark glass cards
(near-black, ~90 % opaque, radius ~24 px at 1080 wide, soft shadow), flat icons in coloured circles,
colour that carries meaning — green gain/us, red loss/them/money, yellow attention/step, blue a chat
bubble.

## 3. Captions

3–4 words at a time, at ~70 % height, white on a soft dark plate, one keyword per group on a solid
block whose colour carries meaning: green (سود، رایگانه، دغدغه، بگیر), red (استاپ، پولی، میلیون),
yellow (everything else stressed: کانال، سیگنال، ChatGPT، سایت). Numbers in Persian digits.

## 4. Sound

| | Reference | Ours (automatic, 142 s) |

| Integrated loudness | −14.0 LUFS | −14.0 LUFS |
| Loudness range | 1.2 LU | 1.6 LU |
| Pauses ≥ 0.3 s | 0 | 6 (2.1 s) |
| Quietest 5 % of frames | −26.8 dB | −32.7 dB |

The level is matched; the difference is the cut (no pause survives) and a continuous bed under the
voice, ~10 dB below it, that never drops out between phrases. Our line is not the weak part; the
cut and the pictures are.

## 5. What the engine must do (in order)

1. **Content cut** — drop repeats, early asks, rants and false starts; open on the strongest concrete claim.
2. **Card design system** — the primitives above (tile grid with ✓/✗, chip, quote bubble, price tag,
   VS card, browser, chat window, step tracker, end card), one look, with state and persistence.
3. **Sentence director** — one element per sentence, filled from the sentence, linked to the next
   (state change before a new element; pay a setup off later).
4. **Captions** — 3–4 words, keyword blocks in meaning colours.
