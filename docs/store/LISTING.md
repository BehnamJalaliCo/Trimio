## English (Google Play)

**Title:** Trimio — AI Motion Graphics

**Short description (max 80):**
Bring a video or a voice. Get word-by-word captions and pro motion graphics.

**Full description:**
Trimio turns a video — or just a voice recording — into a professional motion-graphics edit from one line or a detailed prompt, the way an editor would, on your own phone.

• Word-by-word Persian and English captions with smart emphasis on key words
• 28 design styles: Liquid Glass, Neobrutalism, Kinetic Typography, Minimalism, Chrome / Liquid Metal, Retrofuturism and more
• Animated elements, icons, charts and price tickers, synced to the beat
• Original, royalty-free sound effects and music
• Silence and filler removal, automatic cuts, music ducking under speech
• A live 0–100% build stream: watch every stage happen
• Full editing: every word's text, emphasis, style, music and cuts
• Up to 4K export, plus hand-off to Premiere Pro, Final Cut and DaVinci Resolve
• An AI director that runs on the phone with no subscription — or your own Claude/OpenAI key
• Private by design: your video and voice never leave your phone

For high-end phones (Android 13+, 8 GB RAM or more).
